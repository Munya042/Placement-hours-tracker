Your privacy

Placement Hours is designed to keep each user's placement records separate and protected.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information used by the app

When you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses Firebase Authentication to identify your account.

Google may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account identifier.

The app also stores information you enter or create while using it, including:

  • Placement shift dates
  • Start and finish times
  • Break durations
  • Calculated placement hours
  • Your placement-hours target
  • App settings

2. How your information is used

Your information is used only to provide the functions of Placement Hours, including:

  • Authenticating your account
  • Saving and displaying your placement shifts
  • Calculating totals and progress
  • Synchronising your data between devices
  • Restoring your records when you sign in again

3. Cloud storage

When you are signed in, placement records and selected settings are stored using Google Firebase Firestore.

Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules are used to restrict normal app access so that a signed-in user can access the record associated with their own Firebase user account.

Firebase and Google may process data as technology service providers necessary to operate authentication and cloud storage.

4. Local and offline storage

Placement Hours also stores a copy of app data in your browser's local storage so that the tracker can continue to work when you are offline.

Data stored locally may remain on that device until the browser's site data is cleared or removed.

5. Data sharing

Placement Hours does not sell your placement records or use them for advertising.

Information is processed by services required to operate the app, particularly Google Sign-In and Firebase.

6. Exports and backups

If you use CSV export, Print / PDF, or Backup / Restore, files may be created or downloaded onto your own device.

You are responsible for protecting any copies that you download, print, email or share.

7. Managing your placement records

You can edit and delete individual placement shifts from within the app.

You can also clear your saved placement history using the app's controls. When you are signed in and online, changes are synchronised with your Firebase record.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect app data, including HTTPS, Google authentication and Firestore Security Rules.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also keep their Google account and devices secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features, storage methods or services used by Placement Hours change.

The latest version will be published with the application.
