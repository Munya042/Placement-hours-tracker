What is Placement Hours?

Placement Hours is a simple time-tracking application designed to help students record, manage and review the hours they complete during placements, internships, practicums and professional experience.

Instead of relying on paper notes or manually calculating hours, the app lets you start and finish a shift, automatically calculates the time completed and keeps a history of your placement activity.

Placement Goal

The tracker includes progress monitoring so users can see how many placement hours they have completed and how many remain.

Current target: 460 placement hours.

The required-hours target can be changed in Settings so the app can also be used by students with different placement requirements.

Key Features

  • Start and finish shift timer
  • Automatic calculation of placement hours
  • Add previous or missed shifts manually
  • Edit and delete recorded shifts
  • Record unpaid break time
  • Weekly, monthly and total-hour summaries
  • Progress tracking toward required placement hours
  • Google Sign-In
  • Private cloud synchronisation using Firebase Firestore
  • Local offline storage
  • CSV export
  • Printable / PDF placement records
  • Backup and restore
  • Light and dark appearance options
  • Completion forecast and weekly pace guidance
  • Placement insights and eight-week activity chart
  • Milestone celebrations
  • Professional placement report for PDF or printing
  • Mobile quick Start / Finish controls

Privacy and Ownership

Each signed-in user has their own placement record. Placement data is stored under the user's Firebase account identifier and access is controlled using Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules.

More information is available on the Privacy Policy page.

Who is it for?

Placement Hours is intended for students and trainees who need a straightforward way to document hours completed as part of practical learning, workplace experience or professional placement requirements.
